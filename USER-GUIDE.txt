# Mighty Party Vanguard Optimizer — User Guide

Unofficial community tool by GamerAviator. The optimizer uses a known 52-card database; verify recommendations against the game.

## Quick start

1. Click **+ Account** and name your player account.
2. On **Roster**, enter current levels or use **Import**. Level 0 means unowned; unowned cards are not upgraded.
3. On **Resources**, enter your available balances and select whether Guild Boost is active.
4. Open **Compare** and click **Calculate both modes**.
5. Choose **View upgrade plan** for the strategy you want. Each row shows the exact recommended final level, rarity, faction, and game card position.
6. Export a backup after setup and after major changes.

Roster and Resources show separate last-updated dates. Editing levels or applying a roster import stamps the roster; editing balances or applying a resource import stamps resources. Changing settings does not refresh either date. Older accounts show Date not recorded yet until their next edit or import.

Plans do not update the roster automatically. After performing upgrades in the game, update your levels and resource balances, then recalculate.

## Roster and level screenshots

Choose **Portraits** (the default) for partial or filtered rosters. Cards are identified from artwork against the known 52-Vanguard database, independently of their positions. Missing cards will not shift other matches. Portrait similarity is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Step 1: align the blue boxes to complete visible cards. Set visible rows and columns, and adjust the grid corners. Crop a short final row separately and set its column count; exclude clipped cards. Layout defaults are suggestions.

Step 2: click **Read cards & levels →**. Card portraits are matched locally, and level OCR runs on your device. If OCR is unavailable, review still lets you choose cards and enter levels manually.

Step 3: use the image tabs and compare the captured card against its suggested known portrait. Change the Vanguard selector if incorrect, and verify or correct the level. Card similarity and level OCR confidence are separate. Uncertain identities require manual selection. Every entry starts unchecked. Bulk selection skips low-confidence card matches, unidentified cards, unreadable levels, and duplicates. Review each selected entry before applying, and select only one occurrence of a duplicate Vanguard. Only selected cards change; other saved levels stay untouched.

**Fixed positions** is an optional shortcut only for complete, unfiltered 52-card rosters in game order. Grothmar is #5, row 1 column 5. Old screenshots from before his release use a different order. Do not use fixed positions for accounts with missing Vanguards. In the current full roster, the last row is Bonny then Malphas. Common-card portrait associations and order have been corrected; existing saved levels remain keyed by name. A fresh verified import can correct older misassigned levels.

## Might history

Expand **Your progress** on Roster to see the might graph, growth since tracking began, and exact saved values. Existing accounts start from their current saved roster on first use of this update. New accounts start with their first nonempty saved roster. Each saved roster change records might. Resources, settings and projected optimizer plans do not count as actual growth.

Recorded changes may include newly owned cards, corrections, and upgrades. Earlier unrecorded might cannot be recovered. History stays separate for each account and is included in exported/restored backups. Clearing site data without a backup can erase it.

## Resources and resource screenshot import

Enter Purple Essence, Order Jewels (blue), Nature Jewels (green), Chaos Jewels (red), Regatta Tickets, and Chariot Tickets as whole numbers.

Use **Import resource screenshot** for a tightly cropped four-resource bar in this order: blue Order, red Chaos, green Nature, purple Essence. Click **Read resource amounts**, review the image and values, correct unreadable amounts, select the balances to update, and apply. Tickets remain unchanged.

Abbreviations such as 160m become 160,000,000, but the displayed game value may be rounded. Use exact balances when available.

Each full bundle of 2,500 Regatta or Chariot tickets adds 14,000 essence to the available budget. Leftover tickets below a full bundle do not convert.

## Choosing a strategy

**Max Might:** a fast best-found resource-efficiency plan. No rarity caps or faction balancing apply. It starts at least as high as Balanced for identical inputs and tries efficient upgrade bundles and short local improvements. It finishes automatically; it is **not a guaranteed global maximum**. Equal results can be legitimate.

**Balanced Account:** strengthens the weakest faction that has an affordable eligible upgrade, while considering rarity progression and high-tier development. Targets:

| Rarity | Target |
| --- | ---: |
| Common | 333 |
| Rare | 500 |
| Epic | 667 |
| Legendary | 833 |
| Mythic | 1,000 |

Targets approximate the 6:5:4:3:2 ratio with Mythic at 1,000, rounded to whole levels. Lower rarities never receive further upgrades above target; existing above-target levels remain unchanged. Once every owned card reaches its target, only Mythics advance. If remaining foundation upgrades are unaffordable, the plan may leave resources unused rather than move into the Mythic-only phase. Faction equality is not guaranteed because available jewels and the owned roster differ.

Mythic might applies a 20% bonus to the underlying Legendary curve. Below level 1,000, the bonus is applied before rounding each card down to whole might, matching the game display. At and above 1,000, Mythics start at 190,080 might and gain 300 per level. Totals and faction/attack-type breakdowns sum those card values.

## Reading the plan

Compare starting might, projected final might, might gained, levels gained, faction might, melee/ranged might, and exact final levels. The resource ledger shows actual upgrade consumption and remaining resources. Essence spent includes converted ticket essence. A plan is a recommendation, not an automatic in-game action.

## Guild Boost

The model applies a 5% upgrade-cost discount. Compare calculates both ON and OFF; **Show boosted plan** selects which recommendations you inspect.

The payback section values saved essence against an editable shop-pack amount and price. The default benchmark is 280,000 essence for $99.99. Guild Boost is modeled at $19.99 for three weeks. The value only represents avoided essence purchases if you would otherwise buy them and perform the upgrades during the boost period. Other perks are not included. Verify actual shop values.

## All In One Guild Spoils

Click **Compare with Guild Spoils** to compare your current budget against a hypothetical addition of 630,000 Purple Essence plus 4,200,000 jewels per faction, priced at $99.99 in the reference offer. It uses the selected Guild Boost setting and does not change saved balances.

The table shows current-budget might gain, gain with the offer, and their difference. Max Might differences are best-found estimates. Gold, gems, VIP points, and other rewards are excluded. The comparison is a snapshot: rerun after changing levels, resources, or boost status. Offers may change.

## Install and offline use

The footer’s **Install app** button has device-specific guidance directly beneath it. Expand **How to install** and choose your device. Tapping Install app opens a native prompt where supported, or expands the nearby instructions. On iPhone/iPad, open the app in Safari and use **Share → Add to Home Screen**. Installation support varies by browser.

Load the app online first. Its app files can then be cached for offline manual entry and calculations. Screenshot OCR needs an internet connection on first use to download its reader; offline OCR availability is not guaranteed.

## Saving, backups, and moving devices

Accounts are saved in your current browser on your current device. There is no cloud sync or login. Clearing only cached images/files normally does not erase accounts; clearing site data can. Private browsing is not suitable for durable storage.

Click **Export backup** to download vanguard-accounts.json containing all accounts. Keep it somewhere safe and private. **Restore backup replaces all accounts currently saved on that device** after confirmation—it does not merge them. Export the current device's data before restoring.

Different browsers, installed app contexts, and website addresses can have separate storage. To move from the original app to the GitHub site, export from the original and restore on the new address. Do not upload personal account backups or screenshots into the public GitHub repository.

## Troubleshooting

- Wrong imported card names: fix the starting row or alignment before reading again.
- Missing levels: view the source image, enter them manually, and select only verified values.
- Zero gain: check owned levels, jewel balances, essence, tickets, and Balanced target restrictions.
- Equal Max/Balanced gains: Max may choose the same plan; the fast calculation does not prove global optimality.
- After an update: refresh/reopen the app. Export a backup before clearing any site data.
- After buying a pack: enter the new actual balances and recalculate; the offer comparison never adds resources automatically.

## Resource history and weekly changes

Expand Resource progress on Resources to chart Essence, each faction’s Jewels, or either ticket type. History starts from current saved balances and records actual balance changes only, separately per account. It is included in backups.

Weekly net changes compare the latest saved balance to the latest check-in at or before seven days ago. A short tracking period is labeled incomplete, and sparse check-ins may span more than a week. Stale balances prompt you to update rather than claiming a current weekly gain. Net changes include earnings, spending, purchases and corrections; they cannot determine total resources earned by playing alone.

IMPORT REVIEW UPDATES
Clear repeated card borders are used to suggest the grid automatically. Check the blue preview boxes, then read. If detection is uncertain or missing, use Adjust alignment to change corners, rows and columns. Detection is a suggestion, especially on cropped or clipped screenshots. Apply saves checked entries and keeps the remaining review open. Continue through the screenshots, then choose Finish review to return to Roster. Leaving with unapplied entries asks for confirmation.

## Chrome capture import

On desktop Chrome, install the separate Vanguard Capture extension. Start fresh capture, then refresh the game and wait for loading. Developer Tools is not needed. Review matched names and levels, choose Select matched roster, and export checked data. The extension matches known game IDs, independently of missing cards or screen positions. New IDs stay unmatched.

Select the correct optimizer account, then Import → Import Chrome capture JSON and choose vanguard-extract.json. Review the source label and values. Select all levels and/or Select all resources, or check individual values, then Apply checked Chrome data. Only checked fields change; other players remain untouched. Captured balances include Purple Essence and three faction Jewels. Ticket fields remain manual pending verification.

## SOVs (Seals of Valor)

Resources has a separate War Spoils section for exact SOV balances. Enter a
whole number or leave it blank if not recorded. Resource progress includes
a gold SOV history chart and weekly net changes. Older backups and check-ins
with no SOV balance retain an unknown value, not an invented zero. SOVs stay
with each player account and are included in backups. They are not converted
to essence or used by either optimizer mode.

Vanguard Capture 0.1.6 exports GuildRoulettePoints as SOVs. Review and select
the SOV row when importing Chrome data. The game may show 14k while the
capture supplies the exact 14,778. Screenshot resource-bar import continues
to update only Essence and three Jewels; it leaves SOVs and tickets alone.

## Correcting an import into the wrong account

Export a backup before cleanup. First restore the affected account's current
levels/resources from its own verified Chrome capture. Then on Roster open
Progress → View exact saved values. Find the wrong import by timestamp and
might, and choose Remove check-in. On Resources open Resource progress →
Exact resource check-ins and remove the corresponding incorrect balance row.
Check the values and time carefully; valid history stays in place. Charts and
weekly comparisons recalculate from the remaining records. Removal never
changes current levels/resources and is not re-added automatically on reload.
Undo last removal is available for each history type during the current app
session. It restores that row only. If the mistaken import was the very first
record, earlier unrecorded values cannot be reconstructed; remaining valid
check-ins become the baseline. Other accounts remain untouched.

Roster → Delete account removes that named account's levels, resources and
history from this device after confirmation. It does not delete other player
accounts or affect the game. If you delete the last account, a new empty
Account #1 is created. Export a backup before deleting if you need a copy.
