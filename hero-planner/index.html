How to get the Squad roster file
  1. Download Vanguard Capture 1.0.10. Unzip it and update your unpacked extension, then click Reload at chrome://extensions.
  2. On your Mighty Party tab, choose Hero Diagnostics → Start hero capture.
  3. After the game reloads, click Play. Wait until the extension says Hero roster captured or Hero catalog candidates captured.
  4. Click Download diagnostics under Hero Diagnostics and load the resulting JSON here.

The extension’s Extract this account button creates a .vcef file for the Vanguard Optimizer.
